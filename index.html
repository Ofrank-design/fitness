Coaching, not just programming

A spreadsheet can tell you what to lift. It can't tell you your bar path is drifting, or that you're ready for more volume than the plan called for. Every Apex session — in person or on camera — is reviewed by a coach who adjusts based on what actually happened, not just what was written.

That's the difference between a plan you follow and a plan that follows you.
