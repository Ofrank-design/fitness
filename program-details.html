What a week looks like

Training runs four to five days a week depending on your schedule and goal. Each block is written two to four weeks at a time, with your weekly call used to adjust load, volume, and exercise selection.

Nutrition is set as a daily calorie and macro target, with flexibility in how you hit it — no mandatory meal plan unless you want one.
